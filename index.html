AI tools for online stores, built by Atomon.

Atomon builds software that helps merchants create and run online shops — including an AI e-shop builder and a Shopify app with a voice-enabled AI shopping assistant.

Atomon is a software company based in Greece. We are building two products: an AI e-shop builder that can turn plain-language instructions into a store, and a Shopify app in which an AI avatar helps shoppers by talking with them.

Request early access Contact us at hello@atomon.si

Products

  • AI e-shop builder

    Describe the store you want in plain language. Atomon helps you turn that brief into an online shop.

  • Shopify AI avatar assistant

    A shopping assistant, as an AI avatar, that can talk with shoppers by voice inside a Shopify store.
