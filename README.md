# atomon.si

Static site for https://atomon.si (GitHub Pages, custom domain in `CNAME`).

The home page is a portfolio of the studio’s apps and games. Project pages:

- `/maylo/` — live on the App Store and Google Play
- `/dare-with-me/` — live on Google Play, 18+
- `/idolingo/course/` — under development (legal pages stay at `/idolingo/`)
- `/mr-atlas/` — YouTube channel is live; the app is under development
- `/rise-of-civs/` — under development, early prototype
- `/shopify-avatar/` — under development
- `/e-shop-builder/` — under development

`/el/` is the Greek home and links to the same project pages.

Legal pages are unchanged:

- `/idolingo/...`: copied from the idolingo repo `legal/idolingo/` (generated there by `scripts/build-legal-pages.ts`; edit there, then copy)
- `/to-paixnidi-mas/...`: copied from the to-paixnidi-mas repo `legal/to-paixnidi-mas/` (generated by `scripts/legal-pages.ts`; edit there, then copy)
- `/falagga/...`: written here (no data collected). Not listed on the portfolio.

`studio.js` only reveals cards on scroll and starts muted loops. It does not load analytics or other third-party scripts. Loops are skipped when the visitor prefers reduced motion.
