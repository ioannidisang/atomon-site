A shopping assistant that talks with your customers.

Atomon is building a Shopify app with an AI avatar that helps shoppers in the store — including by voice.

Early access / waitlist. A public demo or Shopify App Store listing has not been published. Email hello@atomon.si if you are a Shopify merchant interested in trying the assistant.

What it does

An avatar in the shop

Shoppers can meet an AI avatar in the store, instead of only a text chat box.

Voice, not only text

The assistant is designed to talk with shoppers by voice, so questions about products can be asked out loud.

Built as a Shopify app

The product is made for Shopify merchants and is intended to run inside a Shopify store.

Help with finding and choosing products

The assistant is a shopping assistant: it is meant to help visitors explore the catalogue and get answers while they browse.

Made for merchants who want a more natural storefront

Useful when you want customers to ask questions the way they would ask a salesperson, including with their voice.

How it works

  1. A Shopify merchant installs the Atomon app in their store (distribution details will be added once the listing path is confirmed).
  2. The merchant sets up the assistant and the avatar for their catalogue.
  3. Shoppers can talk to the assistant — including by voice — while they browse.
  4. The merchant keeps control of the experience and can switch it off at any time.
