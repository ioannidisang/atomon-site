Build an online store from a plain-language brief.

Tell Atomon what you want to sell and how the shop should work. The AI e-shop builder is designed to turn those instructions into a store, without you having to start from a blank page.

Early access / waitlist. A public demo has not been published. Email hello@atomon.si to request access.

What it does

Plain-language instructions

Describe the catalogue, the look, and the basics of how the shop should work in everyday language.

Store setup, not just a text draft

The product is an e-shop builder: the aim is a working store structure, not only a written outline.

Built for merchants who want to move quickly

Meant for people who have a clear idea of what they want to sell and need help turning that idea into a shop.

Made to work alongside your selling tools

The builder is part of Atomon’s commerce software. It is a separate product from our Shopify avatar app, and the two can be used on their own.

Human review before you go live

You stay in control of what gets published. Early access users should expect to review and adjust the result before a store is launched.

How it works

  1. Describe the store you want in plain language — what you sell, who it is for, and any must-have pages or features.
  2. The builder proposes a store structure from that brief.
  3. You review, correct, and complete what is missing.
  4. When you are ready, you take the store toward launch. Exact publishing destinations and integrations will be listed here only once they are confirmed.
