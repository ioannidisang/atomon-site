Privacy policy

Effective 2 November 2026

Who we are

Falagga (working title) is a game published by KOOQIE P.C. Registered office: KOOQIE P.C., 68 Halkidikis St, 11855 Athens, Greece. For privacy questions, write to privacy@kooqie.com.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, and no in-app purchases. It does not send data over the network to us or to anyone else, and it does not ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone.

What stays on your device

Settings such as language, and your progress if the game saves it, are stored only on your device. We cannot see them. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data removes them.

Children

The game does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

App stores

When you download the game, Google Play or the App Store process data under their own privacy policies. We do not receive personal data from them about you.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing to access, correct, or delete on our side. You can still contact us at the address above, or complain to the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (www.dpa.gr).

Changes

If a future version starts collecting data, we will update this page before that version is released and change the date above.
